Shortcuts worth learning

Last digit of a perfect square

The last digit of the number tells you the last digit of its square root.

Number ends in014569
Root ends in01 or 92 or 854 or 63 or 7

Squares never end in 2, 3, 7 or 8.

Last digit of a perfect cube

Each digit maps to exactly one cube-root digit, so there is no guessing.

Cube ends in1234567890
Root ends in1874563290
Finding the tens digit

Compare the number with squares (or cubes) of 10, 20, 30 and so on. For √1764: 40² = 1600 and 50² = 2500, so the root is in the forties. The number ends in 4, so the root ends in 2 or 8. That gives 42 or 48, and 42² = 1764.

Estimating a square root

Find the two perfect squares around the number. For √200: 14² = 196 and 15² = 225. The midpoint between them is about 14 × 15 = 210, and 200 is below it, so the answer rounds to 14.
